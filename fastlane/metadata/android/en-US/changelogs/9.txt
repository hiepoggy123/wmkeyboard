The toolbar's cursor tools do more. Hold one to repeat its move, and press shift
first to extend the selection instead, the way shift+arrow does on a hardware
keyboard.

The layout editor sizes keys and rows with a slider or an exact typed value
rather than in quarter steps, and can scale a row to fit the grid.

Settings screens more than two deep draw a tappable path strip, so you can go
back several levels at once.
