The Typing screen is the largest single screen in Settings, with eight groups covering everything from autocorrect confidence to physical-keyboard shortcuts, plus one sub-screen (the tool-shortcuts letter list) a tap deeper. Most of what's here also has its own task-focused guide, linked from each group below; this page is the field-by-field reference to check a default or find a setting by name.

## Automatic corrections

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Autocorrect | Toggle | On | always |
| Autocorrect confidence | Slider, ×1.5–×10 | ×4.0 | Autocorrect on |
| Undo autocorrect with backspace | Toggle | On | Autocorrect on |
| Skip all-caps words | Toggle | On | Autocorrect on |
| Block offensive words | Toggle | On | Autocorrect on |
| Fix missing apostrophes | Toggle | On | always |
| Auto-capitalize | Toggle | On | always |
| Double-space period | Toggle | On | always |
| Double-space tab | Toggle | Off | always |
| Auto-space after punctuation | Toggle | Off | always |
| Space after a suggestion | Toggle | On | always |
| Wrap selection with brackets | Toggle | On | always |
| Shift re-cases selection | Toggle | On | always |

**Autocorrect** is this group's master switch: the confidence slider and the next three toggles only appear while it's on. **Double-space tab** takes priority over **Double-space period** when both are on. **Space after a suggestion** lives in this group even though it governs the suggestion strip, not autocorrect. It's grouped here in the app's own settings screen, not under Suggestions below.

:::note
**Two settings share the label "Swipe down to hide."** This one, under Typing → Gestures, dismisses the keyboard on a downward swipe of the **spacebar**. A separate, independent toggle under <SettingsPath path="Appearance / Toolbar / Swipe down to hide" /> does the same for a downward flick on the **toolbar** strip instead. See [Gestures](/typing/gestures/#swiping-down-to-hide-the-keyboard) for the full disambiguation.
:::

**Handwrite with swipes** is a two-option picker, not a toggle: it decides what a letter-area swipe *means*, and only appears in the full edition (it needs the bundled ML Kit handwriting model). **Dot leeway after drawing** is the mirror image of **Cooldown after typing**: one guards against misreading a tap as a glide start, the other against misreading a tap as a handwriting dot, and only one of the pair is ever visible at once since they're gated on opposite `Handwrite with swipes` choices.

## Backspace

<SettingsPath path="Typing / Backspace" />

| Setting | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Swipe to delete words | Toggle | On |

A leftward drag on <KeyCap>⌫</KeyCap> deletes a word at a time instead of one character per press. Full writeup: [Gestures](/typing/gestures/#deleting-whole-words-with-backspace).

## Enter key

<SettingsPath path="Typing / Enter key" />

| Setting | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shift + Enter types a newline | Toggle | Off |

With this on, holding <KeyCap>Shift</KeyCap> (or pressing it first on a physical keyboard) makes <KeyCap>Enter</KeyCap> insert a line break instead of triggering the field's Send/Go/Search action. Only a shift you pressed yourself counts: auto-capitalize's own shift doesn't arm it, and caps lock is ignored.

## Volume keys

<SettingsPath path="Typing / Volume keys" />

| Setting | Type | Default | Shown when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Volume cursor control | Toggle | Off | always |
| Release while audio plays | Toggle | On | Volume cursor control on |

With **Volume cursor control** on, volume down/up step the text cursor left/right instead of changing the volume while the keyboard is showing. **Release while audio plays** hands the keys back to normal volume control the instant something is actually playing, re-checked on every keypress.

### The Tool shortcuts list screen

Tapping **Tool shortcuts list** opens a dedicated screen (route `hwshortcuts`) with no reference page of its own, so it's documented here instead. It has two groups:

- **Shortcut key**: one nav row showing the current trigger (a double-tapped modifier or a captured chord), defaulting to **Double-tap Ctrl**. Tapping it opens a capture dialog to record a different double-tap or an arbitrary chord live from an attached keyboard; a chord an app commonly claims for itself (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, Ctrl+S, Ctrl+Tab) is still accepted, with a warning rather than a block.
- **Tools**: one row per supported tool, bound ones first sorted by letter, then unbound ones. Each row shows the tool's icon, name, and its assigned letter (or "—"); a tool turned off under [Tools](/reference/settings/tools/) shows a "Turned off in Tools" caption instead. Tapping a row opens a capture dialog for a new letter, with a collision warning if that letter is already taken.

A **Reset to defaults** button at the bottom restores every letter and the shortcut key itself, behind a confirmation dialog.

<PhoneFrame caption="The Tool shortcuts list screen: every tool with its assigned letter.">
  ![The Tool shortcuts list screen: every tool with its assigned letter.](@assets/screens/reference/settings/typing-hwshortcuts-screen.webp)
</PhoneFrame>

<KeyCap>T</KeyCap> (toolbox) and <KeyCap>?</KeyCap> (cheat sheet) are reserved and can never be bound to a tool. The other 23 letters default close to each tool's own initial. The full binding table lives on [Hardware keyboards](/typing/hardware-keyboard/#opening-tools-with-the-leader-key) and the printable cheat sheet on [Hardware shortcuts](/reference/shortcuts/); this page only covers the settings screen itself.

## Details & edge cases

- **"Space after a suggestion" sits in the wrong-sounding group on purpose.** It reads like a Suggestions setting, but the app itself files it under Automatic corrections, alongside the other space-insertion rules (double-space period/tab, auto-space after punctuation) it shares logic with.
- **Structured and secure fields gate autocorrect, suggestions, and the smart key-hit bias together, but smart chips use a related, separate check.** Autocorrect, suggestions, and the smart key-hit bias all share one check keyed on field kind: password fields and every keypad-style field (email, URL, number, phone, date, time, date-time) lose all three, regardless of how each toggle is set. Smart chips are gated by whether the app has asked to hide the suggestion strip instead, so with **Suggestions in every field** on (the default), a calc or currency chip can still appear in an email or URL field even though autocorrect and suggestions stay off there; number, phone, date, and password fields still block chips either way.
- **Two settings named "Swipe down to hide" are genuinely independent**: the spacebar one here under Gestures, and the toolbar one under [Appearance](/reference/settings/appearance/). Turning one on has no effect on the other.
- **Handwrite with swipes only exists in the full edition**, since it needs the bundled ML Kit handwriting model; the lite edition's Gestures group goes straight from Gesture typing to Glide across spacebar.
- **The Tool shortcuts list route isn't reachable by deep link.** Unlike Typing itself, it doesn't appear in the app's `wmkeyboard://settings/` allowlist, so getting there means opening Typing first and tapping through.
- **Smart key-hit detection needs Suggestions on**, not Autocorrect: it biases boundary taps toward whichever letter the suggestion engine's own prediction favors, so with Suggestions off there's nothing for it to bias toward.
