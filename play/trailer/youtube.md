## Thumbnail

`out/thumbnail.jpg` (1280×720, 117 KB — YouTube limit is 2 MB).
Regenerate with `python3 play/pipeline/gen_thumbnail.py`.

## Upload settings

- Visibility: **Public** (or Unlisted — both work for a Play Store promo link;
  Private does not)
- Audience: **not made for kids**
- **Allow embedding** (Advanced settings → License and distribution) — the Play
  listing plays the video embedded
- No age restriction (Play rejects age-restricted promo videos)
- Category: Science & Technology
- Tags: android keyboard, offline keyboard, private keyboard, glide typing,
  bangla keyboard, avro, keyboard themes, open source keyboard, wm keyboard
- Language: English

## Google Play side

Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing → Video: paste the
plain YouTube watch URL (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=…, not youtu.be, not
a Shorts link).
