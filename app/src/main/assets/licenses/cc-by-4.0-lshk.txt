The bundled Cantonese Jyutping syllable inventory
(assets/dictionaries/jyutping_syllables.txt) is derived from the Jyutping
romanization table published by the Linguistic Society of Hong Kong (LSHK):
https://github.com/lshk-org/jyutping-table

Changes: tone marks removed, syllables deduplicated and sorted.
