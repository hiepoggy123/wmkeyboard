The Cantonese Jyutping conversion dictionary (downloadable pack) is compiled
from the following open-source Cantonese data sources:

1. Rime Cantonese (rime-cantonese)
   Maintained by: Cantonese Computational Linguistics Infrastructure
   Development Workgroup (CanCLID)
   Source: https://github.com/rime/rime-cantonese
   License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
   http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

2. CC-Canto
   Copyright (c) 2015-2017 Pleco Inc.
   Source: https://cantonese.org/
   License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported
   (CC BY-SA 3.0)
   http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

3. Candidate frequencies, and generated rows covering standard vocabulary
   neither Cantonese source lists, derive from the Traditional Chinese
   frequency list built from hermitdave/FrequencyWords (OpenSubtitles 2018)
   Source: https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
   License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
   (CC BY-SA 4.0)
   http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The data has been reformatted and merged for this app (toneless readings,
merged frequencies); original attribution and licensing remain unchanged.
