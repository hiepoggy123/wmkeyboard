The animated emoji the keyboard can send are Noto Animated Emoji, published by
Google: https://googlefonts.github.io/noto-emoji-animation/

Nothing of the set is bundled with the app. Each animation is downloaded from
fonts.gstatic.com at the moment you ask for one, and sent as it arrives. The
app bundles only the list of which emoji have an animation
(assets/emoji/animated.txt), generated from Google's own published index.

Changes: none. The GIF is sent exactly as it is served.
